Smart-ID: a real-looking form, and the phone playing along

Same rule as the collar: no technical explainer. The simulated phone shows the process. The form asks what a real Smart-ID login asks for; SK's demo test people are one tap away, and a small DEMO marker stays honest about the environment.

1 · The Smart-ID page, before you start

Country + personal code, like a bank or e-government login. Tapping a test person fills the code. On the right, "their phone": the screen the person would see, simulated in step with SK's real answers. The sidebar card says "Not verified" until it's done.
